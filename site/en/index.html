Your photos, from the memory card to delivery.

Afterdusk imports, culls, develops and delivers your photos in one free and open source application. On Windows and macOS, with no account and no subscription: your photos stay on your computer.

Download Afterdusk See what it does

Version 0.5, for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) and macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel). The installers are not signed yet: Windows and macOS ask for a confirmation, here is how (in French).

01

Import without losing a thing

Insert the card: Afterdusk recognises it and offers to import. Every file is copied, verified, renamed and filed by date and album, with a second copy on another drive if you want one.

Nothing is ever written to the card. Photos already imported are greyed out, and a camera sending its photos over Wi-Fi sees them arrive during the shoot.

02

Cull fast, from the keyboard

One key for the rating, one for the flag, one for the colour, and the next photo is there at once. Bursts are grouped, and Afterdusk spots blurry, badly exposed or closed-eye shots for you.

Zoom to 100% with a click, the mouse wheel or a drawn rectangle, compare two to four photos, show the client a second screen.

03

Develop without touching the original

RAW files from most cameras, JPEG and HEIF: light, colour, curves, crop, automatic lens corrections, dust spots. Rendering runs on the graphics card, and every adjustment shows live.

Copy your settings to a series, keep versions, go back whenever you like: the original file stays exactly as it came out of the camera.

04

Deliver the right file

Templates ready for Instagram, the web or print, cropped to the right format. Watermark, studio logo, layouts and collages in a few clicks.

For your clients: a web gallery where they tick their favourite photos, and PDF contact sheets.

And also

  • A map of your photos

    Each album where it was shot, even with no GPS in the camera.

  • Your gear

    Bodies and lenses, shutter count, servicing to plan.

  • Your videos

    Frame-by-frame playback and clips exported without re-encoding.

  • Your clients

    Records, sessions and linked albums, to find an order again.

What will not change

  • Free and open source

    No subscription, no ads, under the GPL v3, with public code.

  • Your photos stay with you

    No account, no cloud, no data sent.

  • Your originals are untouchable

    Every copy is verified, and nothing is deleted without going through the bin.
